## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an intimate, tactile, and grounded digital sanctuary for pairs—romantic partners, close friends, or kin. Rejecting saccharine relationship tropes like bubblegum pinks, floating hearts, and cartoonish gamification, the visual narrative embraces organic warmth, emotional depth, and architectural calm. 

The aesthetic synthesizes warm minimalism with high-end editorial intimacy. It feels like an open linen journal, physical playing cards made from heavyweight cotton rag, and soft ambient light. Tactility takes center stage: micro-borders evoke debossed stationery, buttons invite deliberate touch with micro-press physics, and typography shifts dynamically between modern geometric legibility and quiet literary contemplation. The interface recedes into the background to allow shared connection, playful discovery, and emotional resonance to take precedence.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural pigments, warm minerals, and tactile paper tones:

- **Primary (`#D97757` - Terracotta):** Represents vitality, warmth, and active engagement. Used for primary user actions, current player highlights, and pivotal interactive prompts.
- **Secondary (`#4A7C59` - Muted Eucalyptus):** Symbolizes growth, tranquility, and harmony. Serves as the counterpart accent for Player Two, mutual confirmations, streaks, and success states.
- **Tertiary (`#8C8275` - Weathered Driftwood):** Provides muted earthiness for inactive indicators, subtle pill chips, companion metadata, and time stamps.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#1E232A` - Slate Charcoal):** Delivers grounded contrast without the harshness of pure black, carrying the bulk of typography and high-priority icons.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Base Canvas: Oatmeal Cream (`#FAF8F5`)
  - Elevated Surfaces & Containers: Warm Linen (`#F3EFEA`)
  - Recessed / Inset Tracks: Bisque Sand (`#EDE7DF`)
  - Hairline Delimiters: Muted Stone (`#E8E2D9`)

Both players possess complementary color signatures across game loops: Player One occupies warm Terracotta accents, while Player Two operates in Eucalyptus Sage, visually converging on neutral oatmeal surfaces during collaborative milestones.

## Typography

The typographic pairing establishes a quiet, reflective atmosphere. **Playfair Display** introduces an editorial, literary cadence reserved exclusively for reflective prompts, daily inquiry titles, chapter headers, and milestone victories. It creates an aura of intentional conversation rather than fleeting digital arcade play.

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides geometric precision, rounded friendliness, and peak legibility for functional interface copy, player inputs, game mechanics, timestamps, and button labels. Numbers are rendered with tabular clarity for scores and turn timers, while conversational question cards utilize fluid optical kerning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is mobile-first, engineered for thumb-reach convenience, intimate dual-screen viewing, and occasional face-to-face table play:

- **Layout Grid:** A fluid 4-column structure on mobile devices transitioning to an 8-column centralized container (maximum content width: 560px) for foldable devices or tablets. 
- **Margins & Gutter:** Built with a default outer canvas margin of `1.25rem` (`20px`) and column gutters of `1rem` (`16px`) to ensure breathable side clearance on edge-to-edge OLED screens.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** Vertical spacing maintains an 8-point rhythmic cadence (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.25rem`). Top headers honor OS dynamic islands and system status bars with `safe-area-inset-top` padding, while primary action buttons strictly float `1.5rem` above the bottom home indicator.
- **Dual-Pane Balance:** Split-screen game viewports partition space into two identical balanced zones (top/bottom or equal horizontal modules), providing symmetric agency for both players.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy, artificial drop shadows or high-gloss glassmorphism. Instead, it relies on **tactile paper layering and warm ambient occlusion**:

1. **Surface Inset (Level 0):** Background canvas (`#FAF8F5`) sits flush. Input fields and answer slots adopt an inset appearance using `#EDE7DF` with a crisp 1px micro-border (`#E8E2D9`).
2. **Card Surfaces (Level 1):** Interactive cards and floating panels use pure warm linen (`#F3EFEA`) bordered by a subtle 1px stroke of `#E8E2D9`. Shadows are soft and tinted: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Elevated Drawers & Overlays (Level 2):** Modal sheets, question dialogs, and game menus lift smoothly with warm diffuse shadows: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.08)`.
4. **Interactive Tactility:** Active buttons utilize a physical spring effect: on tap, they compress downward 1.5px and reduce their subtle bottom border highlight, giving the tactile sensation of pressing a physical linen-wrapped tile.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes softness, safety, and modern organic flow:

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** Standard for interactive badges, online status indicators, action chips, and floating turn switches.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-3xl` / 24px - 32px):** All major content structures, prompt cards, and modal canvases employ generous corner smoothing (iOS continuous squircle curve) to evoke rounded stones or hand-carved game tokens.
- **Micro-Elements (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):** Form fields, avatar rings, and mini game pieces. The continuous curvilinear form removes visual friction, maintaining a soft, approachable silhouette across all surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta (`#D97757`) fill with cream white text (`#FAF8F5`). 52px height, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), with a subtle bottom tone shift (`#C4684A`) for physical press feedback.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Oatmeal canvas (`#F3EFEA`) with a 1px border of `#E8E2D9` and charcoal slate typography (`#1E232A`). Tactile scale compression on active press.
- **Partner Action:** Duet-state buttons that light up gradually as both players hold down their respective controls simultaneously.

### Companion Status Badges & Chips
- **Player Tether Indicator:** Two intersecting pill rings showing live presence. A glowing eucalyptus dot (`#4A7C59`) pulses subtly for "In App With You", and driftwood (`#8C8275`) denotes "Thinking..." or "Away".
- **Turn Chips:** Compact pills (`px-3 py-1`) in muted tones (`#F3EFEA`) framed by micro-borders, indicating "Your Turn" or "Waiting for Companion".

### Game & Prompt Cards
- Generously padded surfaces (`24px`) finished with `rounded-3xl` corners and 1px micro-borders (`#E8E2D9`).
- Card backs display subtle embossed geometric tether line patterns; front reveals utilize Playfair Display headlines for reflective dialogue prompts.
- Swipe gestures respond with soft rotational physics mimicking physical card stock.

### Input Fields & Response Slates
- Recessed background `#EDE7DF` with 1px border in `#E8E2D9`, shifting to Terracotta `#D97757` on focus.
- 16px corner radius (`rounded-2xl`), interior padding `14px 18px`. Generous multi-line line-height for shared letters and answered prompts.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded circular and squircle toggles with soft spring checkmarks. Selected states fill with eucalyptus green or terracotta depending on player identity, accompanied by subtle haptic feedback.
